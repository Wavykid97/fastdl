# Laneway privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Laneway is a download manager for Windows (the "app") with an optional browser extension for Chrome and Edge (the "extension"). This policy explains what they handle.

**In short: everything stays on your computer.** Laneway has no servers that receive your data, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the extension handles

- **Download addresses and the page they came from.** When you start a download or click a link to a file, the extension passes the file's address and the address of the page it came from to the Laneway app on your computer, so the app can download it.
- **Cookies for the file being downloaded.** The extension reads the website's cookies for that file and passes them to the Laneway app, so downloads that need you to be signed in still work. The app uses them only to download that file.
- **File links on a page.** When you open the extension's "This page" tab, it reads the links on the current page to list the files you can download. Nothing is kept or sent anywhere unless you choose files to download.
- **Your settings** (on/off, skipped sites, notifications) are saved in your browser.

The extension communicates only with the Laneway app on the same computer, through the browser's native messaging. It does not send any of this information to the developer or to anyone else.

## What the app handles

- Your download list, its progress and your settings are saved on your computer, in `%AppData%\Laneway`. The cookies and similar request details a download needs are kept with that download's entry until you remove it from the list.
- The app connects to the websites you download from, and to GitHub to check for Laneway updates. Update checks send no personal information beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address, which GitHub receives.

## Sharing

Laneway does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone, and does not use it for anything other than downloading the files you ask for.

## Removing your information

Remove downloads from Laneway's list to delete their entries. Uninstalling Laneway (Settings > Apps) offers to remove its list and settings; files you downloaded are never deleted. Removing the extension from your browser deletes its settings.
