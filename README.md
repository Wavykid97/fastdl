## What it does

Many servers limit how fast each connection can go. Laneway splits a file into parts and downloads them side by side, so big downloads finish sooner. You can watch each connection fill its part of the bar.

- **Faster downloads**: up to 16 connections per file, and connections that finish early help out the slower ones.
- **Picks up where it stopped**: if your internet drops, Laneway retries and carries on. Even after a crash or power cut, unfinished downloads continue next time.
- **Works with your browser**: with the extension, downloads from Chrome and Edge go to Laneway automatically, including ones that need you to be signed in. Turn it off with one click, or skip sites you'd rather leave to the browser.
- **Download everything on a page**: the extension lists every video, song, zip and document on the page you're on. Tick what you want and send it all at once.
- **Extract zips**: unzip finished `.zip`, `.tar` and `.tar.gz` files with one click, or automatically.
- **Your look**: light or dark, and six accent colours.
- **Keeps itself up to date**: updates install by themselves, and every update is checked to make sure it's genuine.

## Getting started

### Windows
1. [Download Laneway for Windows](https://github.com/Wavykid97/fastdl/releases/latest/download/Laneway-Setup.exe) and run it.
2. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears because Laneway isn't signed with a paid certificate.
3. Click **Install**. Laneway installs just for you, so no administrator permission is needed.

### Mac
1. [Download Laneway for Mac](https://github.com/Wavykid97/fastdl/releases/latest/download/Laneway.dmg) and open it.
2. Drag **Laneway** onto **Applications**.
3. The first time you open it, macOS may block it because it isn't from the App Store. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.

Laneway runs on both Apple-silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or newer). On a Mac it opens in a Chrome, Edge or Brave window, or in Safari if you have none of those.

### Browser extension
[Add Laneway to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/laneway/fkolcpnndjmmnlgagmobpknjhcnfnkdi). It also works in Edge: on the store page, click **Allow extensions from other stores** first. Pin it to your toolbar to switch it on and off and see your downloads.

## Laneway Pro

Laneway is free. A free **activation code** unlocks Pro:

| Free | Pro |
|---|---|
| Up to 4 connections per download | 8 and 16 connections |
| Everything else | Extract zips |
| Automatic light or dark look | Themes, accent colours, colourful connections |

To activate, click **FREE** next to the Laneway name in the app and paste your code.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your computer. Laneway has no accounts, ads or tracking, and the extension only talks to the Laneway app on the same computer. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
